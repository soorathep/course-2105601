Linear ODE systems and the matrix exponential: derivations

Five derivations for Session 3. About two hours; do them before the midterm.

How to use this set

Work each problem on paper before you look at the final answer. The final answer lets you check your result; it does not show the derivation, which is the point of the exercise. These problems are in the style of the midterm.

Problem 1 · A reversible pair by eigenvectors

Solve A ⇌ B with forward constant k1, backward constant k2, a(0) = 1, b(0) = 0, by writing ẋ = Kx and expanding x(0) in the eigenvectors of K. Give the eigenvalues and the time constant of the approach to equilibrium.

Final answer. λ = 0 (equilibrium (k2, k1)/(k1 + k2)) and λ = −(k1 + k2); a(t) = [k2 + k1e−(k₁+k₂)t]/(k1 + k2); time constant 1/(k1 + k2).

Problem 2 · The defective case of A → B → C

For A → B → C with k1 = k2 = k, show that the matrix has a single eigenvector, solve for b(t), and find the time and value of its maximum. Check your answer as the limit k2 → k1 of the general formula.

Final answer. b(t) = kt e−kt; maximum at t = 1/k with b = 1/e ≈ 0.368.

Problem 3 · A damped oscillator as a first-order system

Write ẍ + 2ζωnẋ + ωn²x = 0 as ẏ = Ay. Find the eigenvalues, the damped frequency and the decay time, and evaluate them for ζ = 0.2, ωn = 1.

Final answer. λ = −ζωn ± iωn√(1 − ζ²); for ζ = 0.2, ωn = 1: λ = −0.2 ± 0.980i, damped period 6.41, decay time 5.

Problem 4 · Three tanks in series

Derive the transfer function from feed to outlet of three equal mixed tanks with time constant τ each, and its unit-step response.

Final answer. G(s) = 1/(τs + 1)³; c3(t) = 1 − e−t/τ[1 + t/τ + t²/(2τ²)].

Problem 5 · Detailed balance around a cycle

For the triangle network 1 ⇌ 2 ⇌ 3 ⇌ 1, show that detailed balance requires k12k23k31 = k21k32k13 (Wegscheider’s condition), and verify it for the Lab 3 constants, which are built as kij = cij/xi* with cij = cji.

Final answer. From kijxi* = kjixj* for each pair, the product of the three ratios kij/kji = xj*/xi* around the cycle is 1. With kij = cij/xi*, both products equal c12c23c31/(x1*x2*x3*).
