Nonlinear ODEs: linearization, stability and the phase plane: derivations

Five derivations for Session 4. About two hours; do them before the midterm.

How to use this set

Work each problem on paper before you look at the final answer. The final answer lets you check your result; it does not show the derivation, which is the point of the exercise. These problems are in the style of the midterm.

Problem 1 · From the balances to three groups

Starting from the dimensional mass and energy balances of a cooled CSTR with a first-order Arrhenius reaction and coolant at the feed temperature, derive the dimensionless model used in this session. State each group and the approximation that removes γ from the model.

Final answer. Da = Vk0e−γ/q, B = γ(−ΔH)Cf/(ρcpTf), β = UA/(qρcp); the Frank-Kamenetskii approximation ex₂/(1 + x₂/γ) ≈ ex₂ for γ → ∞.

Problem 2 · Classify by trace and determinant

Classify the origin of ẋ = −x + 2y, ẏ = −3x − y + αy² for α = 0 and explain why α does not change the result. Then classify ẋ = y, ẏ = −x + μy for μ = −0.1, 0 and 0.1, and say for which μ linearization is inconclusive.

Final answer. First system: tr J = −2, det J = 7, (tr J)² < 4 det J: stable focus for any α (the quadratic term does not enter J at the origin). Second: stable focus, center, unstable focus; μ = 0 (a center) is the inconclusive case.

Problem 3 · The slope condition

Show that on the steady-state branch of the cooled CSTR det J = (R′ − G′)/(1 − x1), with G = Bx1(x2) and R = (1 + β)x2. Explain why G′ < R′ is necessary but not sufficient for stability.

Final answer. G′ = Bx1(1 − x1); det J = [(1 + β) − Bx1(1 − x1)]/(1 − x1). G′ < R′ gives det J > 0, but stability also needs tr J = −1/(1 − x1) − (1 + β) + Bx1 < 0.

Problem 4 · A Lyapunov equation

For A = [[−1, 4], [0, −2]] solve AᵀP + PA = −I by hand and show that P is positive definite. Then show that V = |x|² is not a Lyapunov function for this A.

Final answer. P = [[1/2, 2/3], [2/3, 19/12]], eigenvalues 0.183 and 1.90. d|x|²/dt = xᵀ(A + Aᵀ)x, and A + Aᵀ has eigenvalues −7.12 and 1.12, so |x| grows along some directions.

Problem 5 · No cycle anywhere (Bendixson)

Show that ẋ = −x + y², ẏ = −y + x² has no periodic orbit anywhere in the plane, using the divergence of the vector field. Then find its steady states and classify them.

Final answer. div f = −2 < 0 everywhere, so by Bendixson’s criterion there is no closed orbit. Steady states (0, 0), a stable node (J = −I), and (1, 1), a saddle (det J = 1 − 4 = −3).
