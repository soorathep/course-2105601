Vector spaces and the four fundamental subspaces: derivations

Six derivations for Session 1. About two hours; do them before the midterm.

How to use this set

Work each problem on paper before you look at the final answer. The final answer lets you check your result; it does not show the derivation, which is the point of the exercise. These problems are in the style of the midterm.

Problem 1 · Subspaces of function spaces

Which of these are subspaces, and why? (a) {f ∈ C[0, 1] : f(0) = 2f(1)}; (b) {f ∈ C[0, 1] : f(0) = 1}; (c) the 2 × 2 symmetric matrices. For each subspace give its dimension or say that it is infinite.

Final answer. (a) yes, infinite-dimensional; (b) no, it does not contain the zero function; (c) yes, dimension 3, basis [[1, 0], [0, 0]], [[0, 0], [0, 1]], [[0, 1], [1, 0]].

Problem 2 · The derivative as a matrix

Write the matrix D of d/dx on polynomials of degree ≤ 3 in the basis 1, x, x², x³. Give its rank, a basis of its null space and of its column space, and explain both in terms of calculus.

Final answer. D = [[0, 1, 0, 0], [0, 0, 2, 0], [0, 0, 0, 3], [0, 0, 0, 0]]; rank 3; null space spanned by 1 (constants); column space = polynomials of degree ≤ 2 (no derivative has an x³ term).

Problem 3 · The four subspaces of a small matrix

For M = [[1, 2, 0], [2, 4, 1]], find the dimension and a basis of each of the four fundamental subspaces, and check rank–nullity and the orthogonality of N(M) and C(Mᵀ).

Final answer. rank 2; C(Mᵀ) spanned by (1, 2, 0) and (2, 4, 1); N(M) spanned by (−2, 1, 0); C(M) = ℝ²; N(Mᵀ) = {0}. 2 + 1 = 3 columns, and (−2, 1, 0) is orthogonal to both rows.

Problem 4 · Gram–Schmidt on monomials

Apply Gram–Schmidt to 1, x, x² with the inner product ⟨f, g⟩ = ∫−11 fg dx (without normalizing). Compare with the Legendre polynomials.

Final answer. 1, x, x² − 1/3; up to scaling these are P0, P1 and P2 = (3x² − 1)/2.

Problem 5 · Weighted reconciliation from a Lagrangian

Minimize (n − m)ᵀΣ⁻¹(n − m) subject to An = b with a Lagrange multiplier vector, and derive n̂ = m − ΣAᵀ(AΣAᵀ)⁻¹(Am − b). Show that the adjustment n̂ − m is orthogonal to N(A) in the inner product weighted by Σ⁻¹.

Final answer. Stationarity gives Σ⁻¹(n − m) + Aᵀλ = 0, so n = m − ΣAᵀλ; the constraint gives λ = (AΣAᵀ)⁻¹(Am − b). For any v in N(A), vᵀΣ⁻¹(n̂ − m) = −vᵀAᵀλ = −(Av)ᵀλ = 0.

Problem 6 · Independent reactions of a Fischer–Tropsch subset

For the species CO, H2, CH4, H2O, CO2 and C2H6, write the atom matrix, find its rank, the number of independent reactions, and one set of independent reactions.

Final answer. rank 3 (C, H, O are independent), so 6 − 3 = 3 independent reactions, for example CO + 3H2 → CH4 + H2O, CO + H2O → CO2 + H2, 2CO + 5H2 → C2H6 + 2H2O.
